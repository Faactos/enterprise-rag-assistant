# Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Acme
Corporation ("Client") and Northwind Consulting LLC ("Supplier") and governs all
services ordered under it.

## 1. Definitions

"Deliverable" means any work product the Supplier is required to deliver under a
Statement of Work. "Statement of Work" or "SOW" means a document executed by both
parties describing specific services. "Acceptance" means the Client's written
confirmation that a Deliverable conforms to its specification.
## 2. Scope of Services

The Supplier shall provide the professional services described in each Statement
of Work executed under this Agreement. Services within scope comprise:

(a) software design, development and integration work specified in an SOW;
(b) technical advisory and architecture review services;
(c) knowledge transfer and documentation for each Deliverable; and
(d) defect remediation for a period of ninety (90) days following Acceptance.

The following are expressly outside the scope of services and require a separate
written agreement: ongoing managed hosting, twenty-four-hour production support,
procurement of third-party licences on the Client's behalf, and any work at a
site other than the Supplier's own premises.

No change to the scope of services is binding unless recorded in a change order
signed by an authorised representative of each party.
## 3. Fees and Payment

Fees are stated in each SOW and are exclusive of value added tax. The Supplier
shall invoice monthly in arrears. The Client shall pay each undisputed invoice
within thirty (30) days of receipt. Interest accrues on overdue amounts at two
per cent (2%) above the base rate.

## 4. Term and Termination

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term
of twenty-four (24) months. Either party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW
for convenience on sixty (60) days' written notice. Either party may terminate
immediately on written notice if the other commits a material breach that is not
remedied within thirty (30) days of notice of that breach.

## 5. Limitation of Liability

Neither party's aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement shall exceed
the total fees paid under the applicable SOW in the twelve (12) months preceding
the event giving rise to the claim. Nothing limits liability for death or
personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud.
