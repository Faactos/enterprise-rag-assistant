# Information Security Policy

## 1. Access Control

Access to Acme systems is granted on the principle of least privilege. Every
account is individually attributable; shared or generic accounts are prohibited.
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all remote access and for any
administrative account, without exception.

Access rights are reviewed quarterly by the system owner. Rights are revoked
within twenty-four (24) hours of an employee's departure.
## 2. Data Classification and Retention

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Restricted
data must be encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.2 or
later.

Customer records are retained for seven (7) years after the end of the
contractual relationship, after which they are deleted or irreversibly
anonymised. Backup copies are retained for ninety (90) days.

## 3. Incident Response

Suspected security incidents must be reported to the security team within one
(1) hour of discovery. The security team acknowledges within one hour and
classifies severity within four hours. Personal data breaches are notified to
the relevant supervisory authority within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming
aware of them.
