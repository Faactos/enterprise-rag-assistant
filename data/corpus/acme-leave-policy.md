# Acme Corporation Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy sets out the paid and unpaid leave entitlements of all Acme
Corporation employees. It applies to every employee of the company and its
wholly owned subsidiaries, whether full-time, part-time or on a fixed-term
contract. Contractors engaged through an agency are covered by their agency's
own terms and are outside the scope of this policy.
## 2. Annual Leave

Full-time employees accrue twenty-five (25) days of paid annual leave per
calendar year, in addition to public holidays observed in their country of
employment. Part-time employees accrue annual leave on a pro-rata basis
calculated against their contracted weekly hours.

Leave accrues monthly at a rate of 2.08 days per completed month of service.
New joiners accrue from their first day of employment; leave may be taken
before it has accrued only with the written approval of a line manager.

### 2.1 Carry-Over

Employees may carry over a maximum of five (5) unused annual leave days into
the following calendar year. Carried-over days must be taken by 31 March, after
which they lapse without payment. Carry-over above five days requires the
written approval of a department head and is granted only where business
demands prevented the employee from taking the leave.
## 3. Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to ten (10) days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
A medical certificate is required for any absence exceeding three (3)
consecutive working days. Unused sick leave does not carry over and is not paid
out on termination.

## 4. Parental Leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to sixteen (16) weeks of paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers are entitled to four (4) weeks of paid parental leave.
Both entitlements require six months of continuous service at the expected date
of birth or placement.

## 5. Unpaid Leave

Unpaid leave of up to three months may be granted at the company's discretion.
Benefits other than statutory entitlements are suspended for the duration.
